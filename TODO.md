### Lagoa do Fogo (São Miguel): access rules
- Since 2023 the road between Caldeira Velha and Casa da Água has closed to private cars from **15 June to 30 September**, roughly 9:00–19:00. A shuttle runs a loop from Caldeira Velha (Bela Vista viewpoint, Lagoa do Fogo viewpoint, Pico da Barrosa, the trail at Casa da Água). In 2024 it cost about **€5 for non-residents** and was free for residents. Taxis and licensed tour operators are exempt.
- **Not confirmed:** the official 2026 season dates, fare and timetable. One travel blog says the restriction continues in 2026. I found no daily visitor cap, only the shuttle regime.

### Alfama: manageable without steep climbs?
- **Honest answer: not entirely.** Alfama is built on a hillside, and its lanes are steep, cobbled and often stepped. I added a candid note to the blurb (effort: *moderate*).
- What helps: take tram 28 or a taxi to the top (Portas do Sol / Santa Luzia) and walk downhill. The free municipal lifts also help: the Elevador do Castelo (two sections, from Rua dos Fanqueiros in the Baixa up toward the castle), the Santa Luzia lift (from Rua Norberto de Araújo), and a newer lift between Campo das Cebolas and the Sé.
- **Not confirmed:** current lift opening hours and whether each lift is running. Those details come from blogs and forums, not Carris or the city council.
- Sources: [Fodor's forum](https://www.fodors.com/community/europe/how-to-avoid-some-of-the-hills-in-lisbon-chiado-and-alfama-1716896/) · [Lisboa Para Pessoas](https://lisboaparapessoas.pt/en/?p=8104) · [Time Out](https://www.timeout.pt/lisboa/pt/coisas-para-fazer/elevador-da-se)
